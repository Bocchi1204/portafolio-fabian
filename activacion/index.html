Install Office. Activate your license.

  1. Download Office

    Use the Microsoft download below. This installer is Office Professional Plus 2021 for Windows, in Spanish. Your license must match this edition.

    Download Office 2021

  2. Mount and install

    Right-click ProPlus2021Retail.img and choose Mount. Open the new drive, run Setup.exe and wait for installation to finish.

  3. Enter your product key

    Open Word from Start. In the activation window, enter the product key you received with your purchase and select Activate Office.

  4. Find your installation ID

    If the wizard offers activation by telephone, select it and choose your country or region. Keep the installation ID shown on screen.

  5. Get the confirmation ID

    Enter your product key and installation ID in the activation form on this page, then select Get confirmation ID.

  6. Complete activation

    Return to the Office wizard, enter the confirmation ID in its corresponding groups and continue. In File > Account, check that Office shows Product Activated.

Windows blocks the installer?
  1. Open Settings > Privacy & security > Windows Security.
  2. Select App & browser control > Smart App Control settings.
  3. Choose Off and confirm the Windows prompt.
